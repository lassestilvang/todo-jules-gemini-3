💡 What: Extract `usePathname` out of the main Sidebar component into a localized leaf component for dynamic lists.
🎯 Why: Calling `usePathname` at the root of a dense navigation component forces the entire component tree to re-render on every client-side navigation.
📊 Impact: Eliminates massive layout re-renders on route transitions, keeping interaction smooth.
🔬 Measurement: Use React Profiler to verify that Sidebar bypasses re-renders during route navigation.
